# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and
harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing respect and empathy toward others.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, unwanted attention, and publishing another person's private
information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code.
They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not meet these standards
and may communicate appropriate consequences.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and in public spaces when someone is
representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the project
maintainers using the contact method listed in the repository. Reports will be
reviewed fairly and handled with discretion.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may respond with a correction, warning, temporary restriction, or
permanent restriction, depending on the nature and impact of the behavior.

## Attribution

This code is adapted from common open-source community conduct guidance.
